About Me

Get to know your mentor and the journey behind the Agapay program.

VG

Hello, I'm Vince Gayem

I'm a 4th year college student at PHINMA Education University of Iloilo, currently pursuing my degree while actively contributing to the student community.

Beyond academics, I have a strong passion for technology. I am a Cybersecurity enthusiast, dedicated to understanding and protecting digital systems from threats and vulnerabilities. I also work as a programmer, building efficient and functional software solutions, and as a website creator, designing and developing modern, responsive websites.

As one of the mentors of the Agapay Mentorship Program, I'm passionate about helping 2nd year students navigate the challenges of college life. I believe that every student deserves guidance and support to reach their full potential.

Through Agapay, I aim to share my experiences, lessons learned, and practical advice to help younger students build confidence and succeed both academically and personally.
