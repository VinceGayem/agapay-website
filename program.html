Agapay Program

Understanding the mission and vision behind our mentorship initiative.

Program Overview

The Agapay Mentorship Program is a peer-to-peer mentoring initiative at PHINMA Education University of Iloilo. It pairs senior students with younger students to provide academic guidance, personal development support, and career readiness preparation.

The program recognizes that college life can be overwhelming, especially for students in their early years. By connecting them with experienced mentors who have walked the same path, Agapay creates a supportive community where students can grow together.

As a mentor, I work closely with my mentees to understand their challenges, set goals, and develop actionable plans to achieve academic and personal success.

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To empower PHINMA students by providing meaningful mentorship that fosters academic excellence, personal growth, and a sense of community. We are committed to guiding mentees through their college journey with care, patience, and genuine support.

🔭

Our Vision

To build a thriving culture of peer mentorship at PHINMA Education where every student has access to guidance and encouragement. We envision a community where senior students actively uplift the next generation, creating a lasting impact beyond graduation.

Values

What We Stand For

The core values that guide our mentorship approach.

💛

Care (Pagmamalasakit)

We genuinely care about each mentee's well-being and success, going beyond academics to support their overall growth.

🤲

Service (Paglilingkod)

We serve with humility and dedication, using our experience to uplift others rather than to boast or impress.

🤝

Companionship (Kasama)

We walk alongside our mentees as equals, creating a safe and supportive space for open communication.
